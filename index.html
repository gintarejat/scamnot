How to use Scamnot

Time: 1–3 minutes per investigation · Limit: 5 investigations per hour per IP address

The five steps
01 · You
Open a case
  1. In Subject of Investigation — Company / Job Listing / URL, enter the most specific identifier you have:
    • company name + country (Nordvik Capital Ltd, UK), or
    • the job listing URL, or
    • the website domain.
  2. In Supporting Evidence (optional), paste what you have: the recruiter's email text, the salary offered, where you found the listing, names mentioned. The more you paste, the better steps 01 and 06 get. Don't paste passwords, ID documents or bank details.
02 · The agent
Run it
  1. Click ▶ Initiate Investigation. The case number at the top of the folder (INV-2026-xxxx) identifies this investigation.
  2. The status line cycles through the areas ("Running personnel OSINT…", "Scanning adverse media…"). The agent is searching the web at this point.
03 · You
Read the evidence
  1. Seven cards appear, 01 → 07. Each has:
    • a badge: ▲ Flagged ✓ Clear or ? Unverified
    • a one-line summary, details and specific flags
    • source buttons. Open them. A finding is only as good as its source.
  2. Read ? Unverified as "couldn't find evidence either way", not as "fine".
  3. The overall risk level (HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW) is the AI's opinion, not a score. Use it as a hint.
04 · You — human-in-the-loop
Decide
  1. Under Human-in-the-loop review, pick ✕ Confirm: Scam / Fraud or ✓ Clear: Appears Legitimate.
  2. A stamp shows your verdict. Only click once you've checked the flagged sources yourself.
05 · You
Export
  1. Click ↓ Download Report (PDF) for the evidence file (case number, 7 findings, sources, verdict).
  2. ↩ Open New Case resets the form.
Reading the results well
Before you trust a finding
  • Name collisions: an adverse-media hit may be about a different company with a similar name. Check the country, dates and people.
  • "Not found in registry" can mean the company is registered in another country. Check the right registry by hand (UK: Companies House; Lithuania: Registrų centras).
  • Domain age: confirm with a WHOIS/RDAP lookup if it matters to your verdict.
  • A clean result isn't proof of legitimacy. It means public sources raised nothing.
Troubleshooting
SymptomLikely cause

Why this layer matters
Know who you are dealing with

Every later control depends on knowing who the counterparty is. A solid KYB check means registry checks with a manual fallback, ownership traced down to the people behind the business, everyone behind it screened, and monitoring that continues after onboarding.

Reading adverse media and deciding whether a hit is really about the same entity is one of the places AI helps most, and Scamnot already does that part. The upgrade is adding deterministic registry, domain and sanctions checks underneath it, so the evidence is reproducible.
